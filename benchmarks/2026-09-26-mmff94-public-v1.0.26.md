# Published MMFF94 stereo-safe quality — v1.0.26 (#637)

Two runs used PyPI `chematic==1.0.26` and `rdkit==2026.3.6` wheels on one
macOS 26.5.2 arm64 host (CPython 3.13.6). The fixed Tier A+B manifests contain
265 molecules, seed 20260801, at most eight embedding attempts, 200 force-field
iterations, and a 20-second CheMatic per-row timeout. Parsing is excluded from
per-arm timing; RDKit's `AddHs` is included. The common Rust geometry/stereo
scorer was identical for both arms and unchanged from the v1.0.26 tag.

Every CheMatic success was independently sound, stereo-clean, and free of
gross clashes. RDKit produced one `internal_error` on row 8 (cyclopentane:
`BFGSOpt.h` linear-search invariant), retained in the 265-input denominator;
its other 264 rows passed the same scorer. The speed
ratio uses only the 264 common successes. These are two sequential runs on one
host, not a multi-host or release-wide MMFF94 speed claim; run-order/load
effects and the different stereo-repair workflows remain relevant. The
between-engine conformer RMSD is *not* an independent conformer-quality
non-inferiority test.

The complete JSONL, metadata, independent scores, and summaries for both
orders are in `validation/results/mmff94-public-v1026-20260926/`. Run
`python3 scripts/check_mmff94_published_quality_evidence.py` to verify wheel
hashes, 265-row identities and statuses, scorer coverage, and summary hashes.
The runs used `scripts/public_package_3d_chematic.py` and
`scripts/public_package_3d_rdkit.py` with the single MMFF94 arms above, then
`pipeline_v2_vs_rdkit_common_scorer` and
`scripts/summarize_public_package_3d.py`. Original command/configuration,
host/runtime, package paths, timestamps, and output hashes are retained in the
two metadata files. Temporary paths there describe the collection environment;
the committed copies are authoritative.

Together with the current-source same-coordinate energy and gradient packet,
this completes the evidence-regeneration scope of #637. It does not complete
the broader A6 quality gates: conformer-quality non-inferiority,
timeout/convergence across other cohorts, and 2,908 heavy-atom MMFF94 typing
residuals remain open. The same-coordinate energy/term packet is a separate
source-built test, not evidence from this published wheel.
