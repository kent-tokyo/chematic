# Published v1.0.30 reaction: newly created product atom maps

The historical 83-case Rust template-map diagnostic omitted newly created
product atoms with their own map numbers. This exposed three-case supplement
checks mapped and unmapped new O atoms, including an independently emitted
second product. It does not alter the ordinary `TracedProduct` API or the
published v1.0.30 crate.

The product template supplies the map number even when the traced reactant
origin is `None`. RDKit likewise gives a newly created atom `old_mapno` without
`react_idx`/`react_atom_idx`; requiring an origin for that label would
incorrectly discard this case. The result is a diagnostic of template-map
identity, not a change to maps on ordinary product molecules.
