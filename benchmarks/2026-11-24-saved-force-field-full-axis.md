# Full-axis finite-difference diagnostic on previously exposed coordinates

This is an exposed-case diagnostic, not an independent holdout or a general accuracy estimate. It tests the three saved RDKit 2026.09.2 macOS seed-42 re-embeddings from `cod-unused-bridge-fix-rdkit-finite-difference-macos-diagnostic-2026-11-22.json.gz`; these are not the unsaved Linux geometries that originally produced the largest reported discrepancies. The exact input and native-extension hashes, the script hash, energies and per-step results are in the adjacent JSON. It evaluates every coordinate component using central differences of each engine's own energy, at steps 1e-6, 1e-7 and 1e-8 Å. Each reported scaled error is the absolute error divided by max(1, |analytic|, |difference|) at the component with the largest absolute error; it is not the maximum relative error over all components. MMFF94 and UFF only are tested.

For `VDCLBUQGYUHKMX-JXAWBTAJSA-O` (index 2615), at 1e-8 Å, the maximum MMFF94 error in the RDKit-compatible gradient is 64,947.01 kcal/mol/Å at axis 44, about 13.4% of the larger magnitude of the two compared values. The existing `gradient_with_consistent_planes()` reduces the largest absolute error across all axes to 0.0715 at that step; at 1e-7 it is 6.62, consistent with a steep near-singular derivative and finite-step sensitivity. The separate prior term-by-term diagnostic identified the out-of-plane term on axis 43; this full-axis result does not independently attribute axis 44 to a specific term. Both engines' energy differences are close on these coordinates, so agreement between them is not independent proof of the shared energy formula.

For `KKEYKMQSFPECHD-UHFFFAOYSA-N` (index 3669), UFF energy is about 6.65e11 kcal/mol. The compatible-gradient maximum absolute discrepancy grows from 7,358 at 1e-6 Å to 274,871 at 1e-8 Å, while its scaled error at the worst absolute axis grows from 6.81e-9 to 7.53e-8. This step dependence is consistent with loss of finite-difference precision at this energy scale; it does not prove that every gradient component is correct. MMFF94 maximum error here is about 1e-5 or less over the tested steps.

For `KESWAXGJMSZDTG-UHFFFAOYSA-N` (index 11977), the re-embedded macOS coordinates show maximum MMFF94 and UFF errors below 2e-5 across these steps. The Linux coordinates responsible for the original outlier were not saved, so this does not explain or reproduce the Linux result. This diagnostic does not change default force-field behavior and does not add boron or selenium parameters.
