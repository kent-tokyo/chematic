The 12–13 rows with fewer raw matches are not automatically missing chemical
products: their *sets of distinct products* match RDKit. Raw embedding counts
can differ when symmetry-related matches generate the same product. Conversely,
the four or five rows with different distinct product sets remain failures in
the published artifact, even if raw counts happen to agree. The script checks
that these categories do not contradict the original semantic verdicts.

contains the pinned oracle outputs and package outcomes:
