# Published wheel development comparison, 2026-11-24

These are macOS arm64 / Python 3.13 development measurements. The 3D structures were used in earlier development and these results are not an independent holdout. The five wheels were downloaded from PyPI for this host. The 20-block descriptor measurement records the three chematic wheel hashes and checks their installed native extensions. The RDKit and COSMolKit installed native extension hashes were separately checked against the downloaded wheels. RDKit 2026.09.2 wheel SHA-256 is `3860ba53505f27cb24d05383027f78e3f82aefd6caf193c217a94d876d91b023` and COSMolKit 0.6.3 wheel SHA-256 is `cb6840a6040565e66a2244cf7becce37b87d17b0fd644aeb517fba0578cbfc30`.

The 1,000-row pinned RDKit.js corpus, 20 rotating fresh-process blocks, all measured samples, output agreement, per-operation timing and process peak memory are in `2026-11-24-v124-published-descriptor-comparison.json`. TPSA, ring count and Morgan radius 2 fingerprints agreed across all five packages under the existing comparison rules. Median parse-inclusive TPSA times per 1,000 structures were 4.298, 4.336, 4.322, 56.188 and 47.514 ms for chematic 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.4, RDKit and COSMolKit respectively. Process peak medians were 54.8, 54.6, 54.6, 98.3 and 122.5 MiB, including imports and every measured operation.

The fixed-count 3D probe selected 20 exposed development molecules by the existing SHA-256 name ordering from `/private/tmp/chematic-new-cod-selected-split.json`, source SDF SHA-256 `6e2f93fb05c8ff49aff24577aa62d3f04f6531b505e12a9f711c9b79cf3ba008`, using 20 candidates, seed 42, 200 MMFF iterations, declared-stereo checks, and geometry screens. The three chematic releases used the compatible API. Their native comparisons ran sequentially, not in rotating blocks. Their paired results are in `2026-11-24-v122-3d-development.json`, `2026-11-24-v123-3d-development.json`, and `2026-11-24-v124-3d-development.json`. Each release recovered 15 molecules and RDKit recovered 13 under the existing RMSD, geometry and stereo recovery rule. Each produced 380 scored conformers on 19 rows, 197 with optimizer status 0. The existing probe rejected one entire row before generating either engine because RDKit reported MMFF parameters unavailable. It therefore does not report independent chematic results for that row. The median per-molecule compatible chematic generation and minimization times on the remaining rows were 0.078, 0.076 and 0.077 seconds. These unbalanced timings are diagnostic only.

The separate 1.2.4 corrected profile with 400 repair iterations recovered 14 molecules and returned 201 status-0 conformers among 380 candidates on the 19 scored rows. Raw results are `2026-11-24-v124-corrected-3d-development.json`. The COSMolKit run uses `scripts/benchmarks/cosmolkit_3d_development.py` and the same named inputs and independent graph, stereo, RMSD and geometry checks. It recovered 16 of 20 and 15 of the 19 molecules scored by the other probe, with 199 status-0 conformers among 400. Raw results are `2026-11-24-cos063-3d-development.json`. These engines use their own embedding implementations and defaults, so a common count and seed do not imply identical search paths. The small exposed sample cannot establish noninferiority or general performance.

The 1.2.4 development 3D run used the published native extension in a Python package directory that had been modified to add the experimental timeout API. Its native extension hash matches the published wheel, but the installed package as a whole was modified. The subsequent COD holdout uses a separately installed and file-verified published package.

The full 3D comparison, per-engine failure isolation, converged-only recovery, memory measurement, fixed scorer and fresh holdout remain outstanding.
