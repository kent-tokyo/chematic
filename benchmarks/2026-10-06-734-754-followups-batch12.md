## MMFF94 energies

Same-coordinate lane, 265 molecules (262 comparable), each RDKit term
from an `MMFFMolProperties` with only that term on
([rows](../validation/results/mmff94-same-explicit-h-energy-per-term-batch12-2026-10-06.jsonl)):

| Absolute difference from RDKit (kcal/mol) | Batch 11 source | Source |
|---|---:|---:|
| total, median | 0.0040 | **8.5e-14** |
| total, p90 | 0.030 | **3.1e-13** |
| total, max | 0.321 | **1.9e-12** |
| angle, max | 0.358 | 1.1e-13 |
| out-of-plane, max | 0.173 | 3.3e-15 |
| van der Waals, max | 0.080 | 3.1e-13 |
| torsion, max | 0.048 | 8.5e-14 |
| stretch-bend, max | 0.0032 | 2.5e-14 |

Causes, each checked against RDKit's source and values:

- **Out-of-plane:** a trigonal centre has three Wilson-angle terms, one per
  neighbour out of the plane of the other two (MMFF eq. 6, RDKit's
  builder); chematic added one. Energies were a third of RDKit's.
- **Angle constants:** RDKit uses `143.9325 · (π/180)²` and the cubic
  constant −0.006981317 (−0.4 rad⁻¹), not Halgren's rounded 0.043844 and
  −0.007; the difference grows with the deviation (0.36 kcal/mol on
  strained conformers). Stretch-bend takes `143.9325 · π/180`. A linear
  central atom takes `143.9325 ka (1 + cos θ)`.
- **van der Waals:** the default energy and gradient cut pairs at 10 Å;
  RDKit keeps every pair (its threshold is 100 Å). The bounded 10 Å path
  stays as the opt-in cutoff variant.
- **Torsion:** RDKit's MMFFTOR lookup steps through equivalence levels
  (1-1-1-1, 2-2-2-2, 3-2-2-5, 5-2-2-3, 5-2-2-5, then the ring torsion type's
  underlying type) and falls back to Halgren's empirical rule; both are
  ported (`mmff94_torsion_term_params`). An iminium N bonded to an aryl
  carbon (bis-quinolinium macrocycles) has no table row. A directional
  `/` `\` single bond is now single for the torsion type (it gave type 0
  on dienes and stilbenes). On 2,958 molecules of both corpora all 336,298
  torsions get RDKit's parameters.
- **Stretch-bend:** for equal end types RDKit swaps the row's constants
  when the first bond's MMFF bond type is the lower one (conjugated dienes,
  2-2-2).

On 980 molecules embedded by RDKit from both corpora, bond, angle,
stretch-bend and torsion energies agree to 1e-13 and out-of-plane to 1e-5
(two pyridine N-oxides). Eight salts differ in van der Waals and
electrostatics: RDKit's `MMFFGetMoleculeForceField` leaves out
interactions between fragments by default (`ignoreInterfragInteractions`);
chematic keeps them. That is left as it is: dropping them would let the
fragments of a salt overlap in chematic's minimizer.

## Bridgehead amines and other saturated ring lone-pair centres

RDKit's SMILES parser (`chiralAtomNeedsTagInversion`) inverts `@`/`@@` on a
three-connected centre without hydrogen whose bonds are single and on which
exactly one ring-closure digit is written. Parsing stays OpenSMILES (as
decided); `cip_stereo(mode="accurate")` now gives RDKit's label for the
spelling it parsed and marks it spelling-dependent (`spelling_dependent`
in Python, `spellingDependent` in WASM, `cip_label_depends_on_smiles_spelling`
in Rust), as cyclophosphazene P is marked Kekulé-dependent. InChI-based
identity checks refuse these labels. `Molecule::smiles_ring_closure_count`
is new.

- 520 / 520 RDKit-written random spellings of 13 aziridines, phospholanes,
  arsolanes, thiolanium, selenolanium and quinuclidine bridgeheads agree with
  `rdCIPLabeler` (v1.0.36: abstained on all).
- Exposed 10k: **4,395 agree, 0 abstentions** (4,394 + 1).
- A pseudo-asymmetric lone-pair centre (RDKit `s`) stays a typed `tied`
  abstention: rule 5 is applied to carbon only.

## Cost of MMFF94 typing

Parse, add hydrogens and type 2,000 rows (instructions, callgrind; v1.0.36
built from its tag on the same host):

| | v1.0.36 | Batch 11 | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| aromatic-written | 1,391M | — | **1,236M** (−11%) |
| Kekulé-written | 1,311M | — | **1,451M** (+11%) |

Batch 11 measured +4.5% and +26% against its own baseline. The neighbour
lookup the typer uses everywhere scanned every bond of the molecule; it reads
the atom's adjacency now. RDKit's canonical Kekulé structure is skipped when
every aromatic system is one ring sharing no bond with another ring and the
MMFF pass accepts it (the structures then only move that ring's double
bonds, which the pass replaces by aromatic bonds). The remaining Kekulé-input
cost is RDKit's aromatic view of the Kekulé-written rings, which RDKit
computes too. Types are unchanged: 0 heavy atoms and 0 hydrogens differ from
RDKit on all three corpora.

## Canonical SMILES

The automorphism check used to prune canonical search took the next vertex
in index order, so on an explicit-H molecule in a reaction product's atom
order it tried every same-class candidate far from the mapped part: 34 ms
per SMILES on the 117-atom product above, 24 µs now. It extends from the
mapped region (an unmapped neighbour of a mapped atom, candidates among the
neighbours of its image). Canonical SMILES are identical before and after on
both corpora as parsed, with explicit hydrogens and in random atom orders
(15,000 rows), and on all 4,320 product sets of BTMR1063.
