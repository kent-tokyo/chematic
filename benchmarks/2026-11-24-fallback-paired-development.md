# Paired fallback diagnostic on 21 exposed B/Se structures

This paired development diagnostic compares the same v3 candidate API, same 21 previously exposed boron- or selenium-containing structures, same 20 one-at-a-time seeds and same 30-second limit with `allow_unoptimized=False` and `True`. The raw JSON lists every molecule and the exact recovered, lost, unchanged-success and unchanged-failure sets, alongside API, reference, selection and script hashes. Stereo, geometry and heavy-atom RMSD <= 1 Å are scored with `cod_robust_scoring.py` and `summarize_cod_candidate_v2.recovered`. No input or RMSD scoring errors were recorded.

With fallback disabled, all 21 calls reported MMFF type-assignment errors and produced no candidates. With fallback enabled, all calls finished and produced 420 candidates, each marked status -1. Sixteen molecules met the specified geometry, strict declared-stereo and RMSD criterion; five did not. The paired transition was **16 recovered, 0 lost, 0 unchanged success, 5 unchanged failure**. None of the fallback candidates was converged, and the result does not imply MMFF optimization or that all other molecules are unaffected. These 21 cases were selected specifically for B/Se and cannot establish a population-wide improvement. The previously reported 2,029-record v3 evaluation used separately selected records and is the fixed candidate's test, not a matched before/after measure on this set.
