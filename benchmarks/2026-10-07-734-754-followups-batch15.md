## Depictions draw the declared stereo

Python `Mol.svg()`, `_repr_svg_`, `depict_data()`, `to_eps()`, `to_pdf()`,
`svg_highlighted()`, `svg_with_alerts()`, `depict_grid()` and the HTML
reports, and the WASM `depict_svg`, `depict_svg_opts`, `depict_data_json`,
grid and report functions, drew the plain layout: a `/C=C\` double bond came
out trans, SMILES `/` `\` bond marks were drawn as wedges, and tetrahedral
centres had no wedge. They now draw the MOL writer's stereo depiction (E/Z
geometry set by reflection, one checked wedge or hash per centre, drawn from
the centre; `chematic_mol::stereo_depiction::depiction_with_stereo`).
Molecules without stereo are drawn as before.

`scripts/depiction_stereo_vs_rdkit.py` turns `depict_data()` into a MOL
block as drawn and has RDKit read it; a row passes when RDKit's isomeric
canonical SMILES of the drawing equals the input's (double bonds the input
leaves unspecified are not compared):

|---|---:|---:|
| exposed 10k | 286 / 1,687 | **1,686 / 1,687** |
| ChEMBL 5k | 317 / 1,670 | **1,670 / 1,670** |

The exposed-10k row left is the bridgehead amine the MOL writer also
refuses in strict mode (decided).

`SDWriter` passed depiction-unit coordinates to the MOL writer: bonds 40 Å
long, y flipped, and as given coordinates they skipped the E/Z reflection,

`F/C=C\F` and `C[C@H](N)C(=O)O` back from it.

## 2D layout speed

Batch 14's clash-relief pass recounted every atom pair and bond pair for
every candidate move. `write_mol` on the first 2,000 exposed-10k rows
(parse included):

| | Instructions | exposed 10k, wall |
|---|---:|---:|
| batch 13 | 353M | 135 ms |
| batch 14 | 6,960M | 842 ms |
| source (with the cage step and the relief after E/Z reflection below) | **625M** | **300 ms** |

Defects are counted by sweeps along x, candidate moves are scored from the
pairs they change (grid lookups around the moved branch), and a hinge
reached through two targets is tried once. The segment test also treated
two collinear, disjoint bonds as crossing through rounding; it now counts
only proper crossings, which changes the drawing of 32 exposed-10k
molecules (straight chains drawn along one line).

## Bridged and caged ring systems

A bridged ring system (two rings sharing three or more atoms) whose polygon
placement leaves non-bonded atoms within half a bond is redrawn by stress
majorization on its ring-graph distances, started from the polygon drawing
and from classical MDS, turned back onto the polygon drawing and kept only
when it overlaps less (adamantane, morphinans, quinuclidine spiro systems,
trichothecene macrolides).

| 15,000 rows | Batch 14 | Source layout | Source, as drawn | RDKit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| clean (no clash, no crossing) | 14,592 | **14,634** | 14,541 | 14,504 |
| with a clash | 290 | **83** | 155 | 276 |
| with a crossing | 316 | 324 | 359 | 402 |
| bridged rows clean (531) | 245 | **287** | 262 | 232 |
| bridged rows with a clash | 212 | **5** | 42 | 102 |
| bridged rows with a bond off by > 20% | 251 | 262 | 262 | 337 |

"Source layout" is `compute_layout`; "as drawn" is what `depict_data()`
now returns, the stereo depiction above: its E/Z reflections and the
terminal-neighbour placement for wedges move atoms after the layout, and a
reflected side can land on the drawing (a tamoxifen-like tetrasubstituted
alkene puts two aryl rings side by side). The clash relief runs again after
the reflections (155 rows with a clash instead of 172); the MOL writer has
drawn these coordinates since batch 11.

A cage drawn this way may still cross itself (one crossing in most
morphinans, as in RDKit's drawings).

## A6: minimization stalled on a stereo constraint

Row 0161 (a penam sulfoxide) stopped at its first constraint-rejected step
(59 kcal/mol/Å, energy 187.8): its embedding is strained so that the force
field would cross the ring-fusion centres. When minimization ends that way
the pipeline now embeds again from up to three other seeds within the time
budget and keeps the first run that minimizes past it with a sound
geometry; 0161 converges (energy 8.2). Every other A6 row keeps its
coordinates.

| A6, Python stereo-safe arm | 200 iterations | 1,000 (default) |
|---|---:|---:|
| converged | 135 | **264** |
| sound / stereo-clean / gross clash (external scorer) | 265 / 265 / 0 | 265 / 265 / 0 |

Row 0036 converges at 1,077 iterations; at 1,000 it is 0.004 kcal/mol above
that minimum, so the default stays.

## Speed checks against v1.0.34

**BioTransformer BTMR1063 on tri(dodecylthio)methane** (the pair that ran
about 175 s on the v1.0.36 wheel): the time was the canonical SMILES of its
4,320 explicit-H products, 162.6 s on PyPI v1.0.36 and **1.83 s** on the
source (RDKit's `MolToSmiles` on the same molecules: 1.05 s). Batch 13's and
14's canonical changes had already removed it.

**`run_reactants` with Kekulé-written reactants.** Batch 14's comparison
counted work v1.0.34 did not do: v1.0.34 returned no products for five of
the eight Kekulé-written cases. On the three aromatic-written cases, whose
products are identical, v1.0.34 takes 57.3M instructions and the source
**41.2M**; with each reactant parsed once and reused, the source costs 40.7k
instructions per product against v1.0.34's 55.7k. A freshly parsed
Kekulé-written reactant pays RDKit's aromaticity once (about 45k
instructions).

**SMARTS `^n`.** Hybridization codes are now computed per atom on first use
and memoized with their H counts, valences and conjugation; 2,000 molecules
× 10 `^n` patterns stay at 294M (v1.0.34 254M with a different, less
accurate model). The rest is RDKit's conjugation model itself.

**Canonical SMILES, explicit-H rows 4111 and 4384** (about 95 ms for both):
about 2,000 leaves are each serialized because their E/Z-marked strings
differ, so orbit pruning cannot merge them; refinement is 40% and the writer
55%. Left as is; it needs stereo-aware pruning.
