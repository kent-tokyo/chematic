## Branch points drawn 120° apart

A chain atom with two unplaced neighbours put them 30° either side of the
chain direction, so its three bonds were drawn 150°/150°/60°: an ester's
C=O ran 60° from its C-C, and the two aryl rings of a tetrasubstituted
alkene drawn on one side (tamoxifen) overlapped. Every acyclic atom with
three bonds was drawn this way in v1.0.36. The two neighbours now go 60°
either side (120° between all three bonds). Each child's own chain turns
back onto the old direction, so a peptide backbone runs as before. Where
the 120° fork leaves a clash or a crossing in a component, the component
is also laid out with the narrow fork. For components of up to 60 atoms,
both drawings are relieved and the one with fewer defects is kept. Larger
ones keep the layout with fewer defects before relief, then relieve it.

When a stereocentre's terminal neighbour (OH, methyl, halogen) was moved
into the widest gap between the centre's other bonds before its wedge was
drawn, the gap's middle could hold another atom of a fused ring, which
stacked two atoms. The next widest gap is now tried. The atom stays where
the layout put it when no gap is clear and its own spot is.

`scripts/layout_2d_quality_vs_rdkit.py` now also counts *narrow branch
points*: acyclic atoms with three bonds, two of them drawn less than 90°
apart.

The source keeps the full comparison only for components of up to 60 atoms
(the large peptides' relief was the cost). Three changes are byte-identical
on all 15,000 exposed-10k and ChEMBL-5k blocks:

- The layout's free-spot and ring-centre tests no longer look each atom up
  in a component hash set (only the component's atoms are placed).
- Neighbour filters skip the same lookup.
- Canonical E/Z pruning (below) also speeds up the canonical Kekulé
  structure the writer uses.

## MOL blocks and depictions without stereo

Python `to_mol_block()` and `to_mol_block_with_report()` passed no
coordinates to the writer, so a molecule without stereo was written with
every atom at the origin. Both now write the writer's 2D layout in Å, as the
WASM `to_mol_block`, `SDWriter` and RDKit's `MolToMolBlock` do. The reaction
SVG (Python `reaction_svg`, WASM `depict_reaction_svg`), the similarity maps
(Python `similarity_map_svg` and `Mol.similarity_map_svg`) and the WASM
depiction preflight now draw the stereo depiction like the other SVG paths.
New Rust functions: `chematic_depict::depict_reaction_svg_prepared` and
`similarity_map_options`. The Rust facade docs show
`with_stereo_depiction(&mol, chematic::depict::render_svg)`.

New tests:

- Python: `test_mol_block_without_stereo_is_laid_out` and
  `test_reaction_and_similarity_map_svgs_draw_wedges`.
- WASM native: `depict_data_json_draws_declared_ez_and_wedges`,
  `svg_reaction_and_preflight_paths_draw_stereo` and
  `to_mol_block_lays_out_molecules_without_stereo`.

## Canonical SMILES: E/Z marks and orbit pruning

Explicit-H rows 4111 and 4384 of the exposed 10k (about 60 ms and 40 ms)
wrote 864 and 1,152 leaves of the canonical search. All 864 leaves of
row 4111 were the same string. The symmetry search pins every atom near
stereo so that no pruned automorphism can move a stereo reference. For a
`/` `\` bond it also pinned the neighbours of the marked substituent: the
three methyls of a tert-butyl on an imine N were never merged into one
orbit. An E/Z geometry depends only on the double bond's two atoms and their
direct substituents, so a mark now pins those atoms. A mark with no double
bond at either end, and tetrahedral centres, keep pinning both ends'
neighbours.

| | Before | Source |
|---|---:|---:|
| row 4111, explicit H | 60 ms | **2.3 ms** |
| row 4384, explicit H | 40 ms | **0.4 ms** |
| `CC(C)(C)/N=C(/N)c1ccccc1`, leaves | 18 | **2** |
| 30,305 audit inputs (parse + canonical) | 4.49 s | **3.21 s** |
| 13,080 random spellings of the corpora's E/Z rows | 6.69 s | **3.52 s** |

Canonical strings are identical on all 30,305 audit inputs. The random
spellings are RDKit `doRandom` SMILES: six per E/Z row of the exposed 10k
and ChEMBL 5k, with and without explicit H. They are identical to the
previous build's, and every row gives one string across its six spellings
in both builds. Test: `ez_mark_pins_the_double_bond_neighbourhood_only`.

## A6 row 0036

Row 0036 (`COC(=O)/C=C/C(=O)Nc1ccc(CCCCC(=O)N2CCN(...)CC2)cc1`) still stops
at the 1,000-iteration budget. This is not a slow tail:

| Iterations | 200 | 400 | 600 | 700 | 800 | 900 | 1,000 | 1,077 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| max residual force | 3.77 | 0.44 | 0.15 | 0.43 | 3.76 | 0.58 | 0.16 | 1e-4 |

The run rests on a plateau near −22.1, crosses to a lower basin between

MMFF94, run on its own ETKDG embedding in chunks of 200, reports
convergence only after 1,400 iterations. The default stays at 1,000 and the
row stays at the limit (decided).
