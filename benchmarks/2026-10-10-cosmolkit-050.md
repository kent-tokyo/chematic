# COSMolKit 0.5 parity record — 2026-10-10

Source: branch `claude/cosmolkit-parity-3` (unreleased, on top of v1.0.40).
Reference: RDKit 2026.03.1. Competitor: COSMolKit 0.5.0rc15 (PyPI, released
2026-10-08, reworked API) and 0.3.0. Harness:
`validation/cosmolkit_comparison/` (COSMolKit 0.5 adapters, 161 operations).
A row counts only when the value equals RDKit's (ints and floats as numbers);
round trips (`*_rt`) are judged against the input molecule. Refusals,
unsupported operations and errors never count.

Corpora: ChEMBL 5k, RDKit.js 10k, and the new stress corpus
`corpora/stress-v1.smi` (4,072 unusual but RDKit-valid SMILES: antiaromatic and
odd-sized rings, charged/radical aromatics, hypervalent atoms, metals and
dative bonds, non-tetrahedral stereo, isotopes, explicit `[H]`, dummy and
superheavy atoms, plus seeded mutations of ChEMBL rows; generator
`make_stress_corpus.py`).

## Correctness

Full runs (ChEMBL and RDKit.js 10k: all operations including seeded ETKDG,
MMFF/UFF energy and gradient; stress: all but the embedding and force-field
operations). chematic matches RDKit at least as often as COSMolKit 0.5 on every
operation of ChEMBL and RDKit.js 10k, and on every stress operation except
`canonical_smiles_rt` (4048 vs 4064; this checks chematic's native canonical
writer, and the rows are metal-centre stereo and charged non-aromatic rings
where RDKit's own canonical output depends on input order). Focused TPSA
reruns reached 5,000/5,000, 10,000/10,000 and 4,072/4,072 after the sign fix;
the full 161-operation runs predate that final fix.

1,270 (the other 3 are refused by both RDKit and chematic).

COSMolKit's stress maximum is 4,066 because it fails to parse 6 rows. In the
ChEMBL MOL-block cases, RDKit's own `MolToMolBlock` flips a macrocyclic cis
double bond to trans; COSMolKit 0.5 reproduces that, chematic keeps the input
stereo.

## Existing SMIRKS fuzz gate

The fixed issue-754 lane was rerun against RDKit 2026.03.6 after the parity
changes (2,000 templates, 16,000 applications, seed 754). The implicit-H lane
is unchanged: 2,482 exact, 28 differing and 18 typed refusals. The explicit-H
classification moved because the RDKit-model reactant path now removes and
reconstructs explicit hydrogens: 1,051 exact, 577 implicit-H-equivalent, 141
differing and 20 typed refusals. These are deterministic diagnostic counts,
not a broad SMIRKS-equivalence claim. The updated counts are pinned in
`validation/smirks_property_fuzz_expected.json`.

## Speed (first 1,000 ChEMBL rows, `bench_corpus.py --ops all05`, median of 3 blocks, idle machine, ms)

chematic is faster than COSMolKit 0.5 on 33 of the 41 operations both offer,
e.g. parse 6 vs 273, canonical SMILES 81 vs 150, logP 84 vs 455, QED 403 vs
1604, RDKit fingerprint 268 vs 1695, stereoisomers 478 vs 4123, canonical
tautomer 960 vs 143430, tautomer set 6114 vs 185938.

Slower than COSMolKit 0.5 (COSMolKit precomputes ring information while
parsing, which these per-operation times exclude):

| Operation | chematic | COSMolKit 0.5 | RDKit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `num_rings` | 8 | 1 | 1 |
| `labute_asa` | 11 | 2 | 32 |
| `tpsa` | 9 | 3 | 14 |
| `morgan2_chiral` | 70 | 28 | 92 |
| `rxn_amide_amine` | 64 | 39 | 75 |
| `mmff_energy_gradient` | 1447 | 1389 | 1559 |

The first four rows come from a repeat run of those operations (3 blocks; in it
`chi1v` and `formula` were faster than COSMolKit 0.5); the
full 41-operation run had shown an unreproduced 49.7 s for `labute_asa`, while
a direct timing of the same 1,000 molecules gives 11 ms.

## Not covered
