## Published-wheel chemistry gates

The macOS and Windows jobs stopped before any chemistry ran:

- **macOS.** `pip install rdkit==2026.3.6` fails on the x86-64 runner,
  because RDKit 2026.3.6 has no macOS x86-64 wheel; arm64 has one.
- **Windows (and every platform).** The rule-table step looked for
  `database_metabolicReactions.json`. The BioTransformer repository stores the
  tables as `database/metabolicReactions.json` and
  `database/ENVMICRO/metabolicReactions.json`, so the step never found a file.

The workflow now has two stages:

- **chematic.** On each macOS and Windows runner, without RDKit,
  `published_wheel_chemistry_gates.py --phase chematic` writes chematic's side
  of every gate:
  - the 83 reaction candidates;
  - the 310k SMARTS cells against the archived oracle;
  - the chemistry dumps of the exposed 10k and ChEMBL 5k;
  - chematic's products for the BioTransformer corpus
    (`biotransformer_chematic_responses.py`, one gzip JSON line per rule and
    reactant request).
- **compare.** A Linux job per platform installs the RDKit 2026.03.6 wheel
  after checking its SHA-256 and runs `--phase rdkit`. It runs the provenance
  gate, compares the dumps and replays the BioTransformer responses
  (`biotransformer_rule_corpus.py --replay`), then checks every count
  against the expected file.

Other changes:

- **Reactant requests.** The BioTransformer requests are the implicit-H and
  explicit-H SMILES RDKit writes for the 400 reactants. They are pinned as
  `validation/biotransformer-requests-400.tsv`
  (`biotransformer_requests.py --check` regenerates them), so the chematic
  side needs no RDKit.
- **Rule tables.** `fetch_biotransformer_rules.py` makes a partial clone with
  no checkout, runs `git show <commit>:<path>` for the three tables and checks
  them against the pinned SHA-256. All three were fetched and matched here.
- **Local check.** Both phases were run on this host end to end, with the
  BioTransformer responses in three rule shards (30 s each). The
  BioTransformer counts are identical to the in-process run.
- **Tests.** One test checks that no chematic-side script imports RDKit at
  module level. Another checks that the replay answers the recorded requests
  and stops on an unrecorded one.

## 2D layout

| 15,000 rows, as drawn | Batch 19 | Source | RDKit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| clean | 14,757 | **14,767** | 14,504 |
| with a crossing | 242 | **232** | 402 |
| with a clash | 25 | **23** | 276 |
| narrow branch atoms (rows) | 1,876 (298) | **1,757 (251)** | 0 |
| bridged rows with more crossings than RDKit | 31 | **14** | — |

29 rows have fewer clashes and crossings than in batch 19 and 2 have more: a
peptide hydrazide gains 2 crossings, and chlorendic acid gains 1. The
changes:

- **Porphyrins.** The pyrroles of a porphyrin share three atoms each with the
  16-membered ring, so the polygon placement folded them inside it. A
  porphyrin core is now drawn from a template: four regular pentagons, inner
  atoms facing the centre, every bond one bond length, 126° at the fusion and
  meso carbons. A metal bonded to the four inner atoms sits at the centre.
  Protoporphyrin IX: 1 clash and 7 crossings → none (RDKit none). The
  adamantane template uses the same path.
- **Straight chains through CF2.** A chain atom with three unplaced
  neighbours spread them as a cross with the chain at ±90°, so a perfluoroalkyl
  chain turned 90° at every carbon and curled back. When only one of the
  three continues, it now goes straight on.
  - Perfluorotributylamine: 2 clashes and 13 crossings → none.
  - Batch 19's perfluorooctyl catechol: no crossing.
  - Both now look like RDKit's drawings.
- **Exterior gaps at ring-fusion atoms.** The angular separation of a
  candidate direction from a placed bond was `2π − |a − b|` when
  `|a − b| > π`. A candidate past 2π (330°) against a negative angle (−90°)
  came out negative, so the exterior gap of a ring-fusion atom ranked last
  and its substituent went into a ring. The difference is now reduced to
  [0, 2π) first. This fixed:
  - triterpenoid C28 amides;
  - the phenylalkyl chain of the oxetane morphinans;
  - a topiramate-like bicyclic sulfamate.
- **Relief retry.** When the clash relief, which refuses moves that stack two
  atoms (batch 19), leaves a clash, it runs again allowing them. It keeps
  that drawing only when it has fewer defects and no more stacked atoms.
- **Tried and dropped.**
  - Refusing directions whose bond would cross a placed bond: 7 better rows,
    20 worse (camphor bridges).
  - Opening a long peptide's narrow forks away from the ±45° drawing's
    defects: no row changed, at 23M more instructions.

New cases in `bridged_and_crowded_layouts_have_no_clash_or_crossing`
(porphyrins, perfluoroalkyls, a triterpenoid amide, a bicyclic sulfamate)
and `angle_separation_wraps_past_two_pi`. `write_mol` on 2,000 rows takes
647M instructions (batch 19: 660M). The fixes leave fewer drawings to
relieve.

## Analysed, unchanged

- **Long peptides' narrow forks.** Relieving the ±45° drawing of components
  of more than 60 atoms costs 165M instructions on 2,000 rows (+25%). Decided
  in batch 19; the cheaper variant above changed nothing.
- **Slowest canonical rows** (batch 19). ChEMBL-5k row 2984 is bounded by the
  planner's 65,536-candidate cap and takes 3.8 ms. The 333-atom explicit-H
  peptide's cost is the generic refinement. Decided.
- **RDKit 2026.09.1 Python.** PyPI's latest `rdkit` is still 2026.3.6
  (checked 2026-10-08), so the Python rebaseline beyond the SMARTS cells
  waits.
