## Reaction products independent of hash-map order

A reaction product's carried atoms were added breadth-first from template
atoms taken from a hash set, and its carried bonds in hash-map order. The
product's bond order decides its Kekulé form, so a mixed aromatic product's
fate under RDKit's sanitize could change with a map's capacity: pre-sizing
one map in the previous batch turned BTMR1032's furocoumarin product into one
RDKit cannot sanitize. Carried atoms are now seeded in reactant atom order and
carried bonds added in product atom order. The BioTransformer corpus gives
the same rows as before (1,051/1,051 implicit-H, 6,604/6,652 explicit-H; all 233 non-exact rows identical), and the pre-sizing is back.

## Lone-pair CIP centres in rings

| Centre | RDKit's reading of the spelling | Accurate CIP |
|---|---|---|
| Ring sulfoxide or selenoxide (`O=[S@@]1CCc2ccccc21`) | as chematic: 180/180 random spellings of five sulfoxides and a selenoxide give the same label | labelled (was `LonePairCenter`) |
| Ring centre with three single bonds (bridgehead amine, cyclic phosphine, sulfonium) | changes with the spelling | `LonePairCenter`, as before |

chematic reads `@`/`@@` on a three-connected centre as OpenSMILES does, with
the lone pair in the implicit-H position. RDKit 2026.03.6 does not for ring
amines, phosphines and sulfonium ions: random spellings of one bridged amine
fall into two groups that chematic reads as different diastereomers and RDKit
as one molecule (worked through by hand for `C[N@]1C[C@H]1C`: two spellings
RDKit treats as the same differ at N under the OpenSMILES rule). The exposed
10k (4,394 agree, 1 abstention) and ChEMBL 5k (4,166 agree, 20
cyclophosphazene `OracleUnstable`) labels are unchanged; the thiane oxide
pseudoasymmetric `s` stays unlabelled.

An aromatic S takes the thiophene type (S5, 44) only in a five-membered ring

## Performance

| Operation | Before | Source |
|---|---:|---:|
| `write_mol`, 2,000 exposed-10k molecules (program instructions) | 406M | 384M |
| 13 reaction cases × 100 (`run_reactants` instructions; v1.0.34 271M) | 317M | 313M |

`write_mol` writes laid-out atom lines without the formatter (byte-identical
on 15,000 blocks). A cheaper deterministic Kekulé order for the 15% of
molecules that need one saved 4% and changed bytes, so it was not kept. The
remaining reaction cost is product construction (adjacency allocation, the
per-atom valence check, ring flags); no cut that keeps outputs was found.
