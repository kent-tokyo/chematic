# #734/#754 follow-ups, batch 23: octahedral metal complexes

Follows [batch 22](2026-10-08-734-754-followups-batch22.md). Batch 18
recorded the octahedral complexes as decided. They were the largest group of
rows left with a clash: a metal with four pyridines and two thiocyanates
spreads its six bonds 60° apart, so at one bond length the pyridine rings
touch. The record noted that a fix would mean drawing the coordination bonds
longer; this batch does that.

## The change

A ring entered from an atom with six or more bonds is placed one and a half
bond lengths out. Five-coordinate centres, such as Ph3SbCl2, spread their
bonds 72° apart and already drew clean, so they are left as they were.

| 15,000 rows, as drawn | Batch 22 | Source | RDKit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| clean | 14,769 | **14,787** | 14,504 |
| with a clash | 23 | **5** | 276 |
| with a crossing | 230 | **213** | 402 |
| bond off by > 20% | 278 | 296 | 540 |

18 rows lose their clash and crossings, and none gets worse. The 18 rows
with a bond more than 20% off are those complexes: their coordination bonds
are drawn 1.5 bonds long by design.

The 5 rows that keep a clash:

- three fullerene adducts (a sphere drawn flat; decided in batch 19);
- a spiro macrolide with 1 clash (RDKit 3);
- an ellagitannin with 2 clashes (RDKit 4).

Test: the nickel tetrapyridine dithiocyanate in
`bridged_and_crowded_layouts_have_no_clash_or_crossing`.
