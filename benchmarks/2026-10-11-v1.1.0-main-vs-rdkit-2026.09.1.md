## Speed

Ratios are `RDKit time / chematic time`; values above `1.0` favor chematic.

| Operation | Prepared ratio, paired 95% CI | Winning blocks | Parse-inclusive ratio, paired 95% CI | Winning blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TPSA | **1.329×** (1.310–1.431) | 20 / 21 | **12.939×** (11.943–13.539) | 21 / 21 |
| Labute ASA | 0.664× (0.651–0.706) | 1 / 21 | **12.686×** (11.823–13.185) | 21 / 21 |
| Ring count | 0.028× (0.025–0.029) | 0 / 21 | **8.554×** (8.148–8.903) | 21 / 21 |
| Chiral Morgan R2 | **1.097×** (1.018–1.136) | 19 / 21 | **4.129×** (4.006–4.200) | 21 / 21 |

Prepared TPSA and Morgan are equivalent-output speed wins on this host.
Prepared Labute ASA and first-use ring count remain deficits. RDKit computes
ring information during parsing, so its prepared ring lane is a cache lookup;
the parse-inclusive lane measures the corresponding end-to-end workflow.

## Interpretation

- All four parse-inclusive pipelines favor chematic in every block.
- The result is one wheel, one host and one exposed corpus. It is not a
  universal speed claim.
- The wheel was built after the v1.1.0 release tag. A published-package claim
  requires a separate registry-artifact rerun.
- Raw samples, artifact hashes, corpus hash, output mismatches and environment
  metadata are retained in the JSON record.
