# Bounded conformer-pool follow-up against COSMolKit 0.5.0

This source candidate starts from `origin/main` at `342d1d5f`. It adds an
explicit candidate-pool count to CheMatic's opt-in ETKDGv3/MMFF94 torsion
search, so callers can return 24 conformers after searching 48 candidates per
seed batch. The comparator is the [COSMolKit 0.5.0 Python
package](https://pypi.org/project/cosmolkit/) with RDKit 2026.09.1 as an
independent same-coordinate MMFF94 scorer. The metric is the lowest
**converged** MMFF94 energy, with differences of at most 0.01 kcal/mol
classified as equivalent. It is a force-field search metric, not experimental
geometry accuracy.

## Selection and work budget

The [previous comparison](3d-mmff-torsion-search-2026-10-24.md) measured the
first 12 of 35 eligible, SHA-256-ordered Tier B molecules. This follow-up
measures the last 11 eligible rows: six at offsets 24–29 and five at 30–34.
They were selected by the existing graph, size, rotor, MMFF-parameter, and
SMILES-hash rule before assessing the pool. The six include the observed
`chembl_tier_b_0074` gap used to develop the API. The next five were already
exposed to a baseline run, so neither packet is a sealed holdout.

All arms use explicit H, ETKDGv3 seed 42, MMFF94, a 100 Å nonbonded threshold,
and at most 1,000 optimizer iterations. COSMolKit generates and optimizes 24
conformers on one thread. CheMatic's quality profile normally searches 48–96
seeds in batches of 24; its existing torsion arm adds up to 24 minimizations.
The new pool arm searches 96–192 seeds in batches of 48, adds up to 24 torsion
minimizations, and returns its best 24 conformers. The larger work budget is
intentional and was **not** timed against COSMolKit.

| Selection and CheMatic arm | CheMatic lower | COSMolKit lower | Within 0.01 kcal/mol |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First six, existing quality | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| First six, existing torsion search | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| First six, 48-candidate pool | **5** | **0** | 1 |
| Next five, existing torsion search | 3 | 0 | 2 |
| Next five, 48-candidate pool | 3 | 0 | 2 |

For `chembl_tier_b_0074`, the torsion arm returned 115.572928 kcal/mol,
COSMolKit 111.870156, and the pool arm **111.666305** while still returning
24 conformers. The pool improved the existing torsion minimum by more than
0.01 kcal/mol on 3/6 and 1/5 rows, respectively. All 11 selected best
conformers converged. The comparator checked indexed atoms and bonds against
RDKit, preserved specified tetrahedral and double-bond stereochemistry, and
re-scored both engines' coordinates with RDKit MMFF94. The largest absolute
same-coordinate energy discrepancy was below 6e-14 kcal/mol.

## Reproduce

With COSMolKit 0.5.0 and RDKit 2026.09.1 installed in one interpreter:

```sh
cargo build --locked -p chematic-3d --example etkdg_torsion_search_probe
python validation/cosmolkit_comparison/compare_3d_torsion_search.py \
  --binary target/debug/examples/etkdg_torsion_search_probe \
  --source-output /tmp/chematic-pool48-six-source.jsonl \
  --output /tmp/chematic-pool48-six.json \
  --selection-offset 24 --selection-limit 6 --candidate-confs 48
python validation/cosmolkit_comparison/compare_3d_torsion_search.py \
  --binary target/debug/examples/etkdg_torsion_search_probe \
  --source-output /tmp/chematic-pool48-five-source.jsonl \
  --output /tmp/chematic-pool48-five.json \
  --selection-offset 30 --selection-limit 5 --candidate-confs 48
```

In Python, `mol.etkdg_embed_multiple_confs_mmff_torsion_search(24,
candidate_confs=48)` uses the new budget. Omitting `candidate_confs` preserves
the previous 24-candidate behavior. A larger pool can improve the minimum but
costs more computation; these packets do not establish a same-work advantage,
better conformer diversity, better physical force-field accuracy, or broad 3D
superiority over COSMolKit.
